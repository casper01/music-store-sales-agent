# Fictional exercise rules: refund policy

> This is an invented policy for the exercise. It is not a real Chinook policy and is not evidence
> that a refund was issued or is available for any specific purchase.

## Request window

A customer may request a refund within 30 calendar days of the invoice date. Requests received
after that window are outside this fictional policy and should be referred for manual review rather
than represented as automatically eligible.

## Data limitations

The Chinook sample database has no refund-request, refund-approval, or refund-payment records.
Invoice data can establish a purchase date and amount only. It cannot establish whether a customer
requested or received a refund. The agent must distinguish the fictional policy from actual
transaction history.
