# Fictional exercise rules: sales interpretation playbook

> These are invented analysis suggestions for this exercise, not official Chinook guidance.

## Interpreting a top-revenue country

Treat country revenue as a signal for investigation, not as proof of market quality or growth.
Before recommending action, inspect the date range, invoice count, customer count, average invoice
value, and whether a small number of customers account for most of the result. Compare periods
when possible and note that the sample database may not represent the full market.

## Customer concentration and limitations

A lifetime-revenue ranking is descriptive, not predictive. State the data horizon and calculation,
check whether the ranking is driven by a small number of invoices, and avoid inferring customer
intent, profitability, or future value from revenue alone. Chinook has no acquisition cost, margin,
refund, or complete customer-activity attributes.
